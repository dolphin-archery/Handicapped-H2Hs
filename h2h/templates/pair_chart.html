How the winner is decided

Each archer's current AGB handicap gives an angular aiming-error standard deviation (σr), which fixes the probability an arrow lands in each scoring ring. Convolving that per-arrow distribution npass = {{ event.n_pass }} times gives the exact distribution of that archer's score over one pass, shown above. Each archer's actual pass score is converted into a percentile under their own distribution -- the probability of scoring at or below what they actually shot. The archer who over-performed their own model by more (higher percentile) wins the pass, which fairly accounts for archers having different score variability, not just different average scores.

Note: when the round mode is indoor, a Compound archer's distribution uses the AGB indoor-compound scoring variant (only the X-ring scores 10); no bowstyle-specific statistical variance correction is applied beyond that, per Specification/humanSpec.md.
