Score distributions

How the winner is decided

Each archer's current AGB handicap gives an angular aiming-error standard deviation (σr), which fixes the probability an arrow lands in each scoring ring. Convolving that per-arrow distribution npass = {{ match.n_pass }} times gives the exact distribution of that archer's score over one pass, shown above. Each archer's actual pass score is converted into a percentile under their own distribution -- the probability of scoring at or below what they actually shot. The archer who over-performed their own model by more (higher percentile) wins the pass, which fairly accounts for archers having different score variability, not just different average scores.

The "equiv. handicap" column converts a pass score into the full-round AGB handicap that would, on average, produce that score -- an intuitive reference number, but a noisy one for a single short pass.

Note: this version does not apply any bowstyle-specific variance correction -- the distributions above depend only on handicap and distance, per Specification/humanSpec.md.
