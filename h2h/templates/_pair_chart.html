Score distributions

How the winner is decided

An archer's handicap sets how well they are expected to shoot. The coloured curves show the scores each archer is expected to shoot, and the dashed vertical lines show the scores they actually shot.

A percentile says how good a score is for that archer's handicap: it is the chance of scoring that much or less. Whoever has the higher percentile did better for their handicap and wins the pass.
